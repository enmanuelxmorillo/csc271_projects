Turn Software Problems Into Reports Developers Can Understand

BugFlow is a web-based application that helps users create clear and organized bug reports without needing a technical background.

Instead of trying to figure out what information a developer needs, BugFlow asks you simple questions about the problem and organizes your answers into a useful report. Reporting software problems should be simple.

Report a Bug

HOW IT ALL WORKS

  1. Tell Us What Happened
    Answer simple questions about the software problem.
  2. BugFlow Organizes It
    Your answers are organized into a structured bug report.
  3. Share or Import Your Report
    Copy or download the report and send it to a developer or support team.

Why Tell Us !?

  • Easy to use
  • No technical knowledge required
  • Creates organized bug reports
  • Helps developers understand software problems
  • Works for websites, applications, games, and other software
